How to export from Mozaik

To get your door list into this calculator, follow these steps in Mozaik:

  1. Open your job in Mozaik.
  2. Go to the Products or Items list.
  3. Select Export Door List (or Cutlist).
  4. Ensure the CSV format matches: Qty, Width, Height, Part, Cabinet...
  5. Drag that CSV file into the box on the left.
